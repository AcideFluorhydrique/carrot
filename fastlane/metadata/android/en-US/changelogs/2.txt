A near total rewrite of the game.

* Two new towers: Poison and Tesla, for five in total
* Five enemy types with distinct behaviour, including armoured units and bosses
* Restructured into 5 chapters of 4 levels each, chosen from a winding trail map
* Each chapter has its own palette, from green garden to frozen plain to volcano
* Towers now unlock as you progress, each one just before the enemy it counters
* Rebalanced difficulty curve, with a breather at the start of every chapter
* Breakable obstacles: mark one and idle towers clear it for gold and a build slot
* Three new towers: Moon (wide group slow), Rocket (piercing line), Sun (ring pulse)
* Each level now offers only 4-5 of your towers, so every map is its own puzzle
* No-build zones behind the spawn and the carrot
* New app icon
* Sell towers, choose targeting priority, control game speed, call waves early
* Particle effects, floating damage numbers, screen shake and a boss health bar
* Sound effects and background music, synthesised on device
* Settings screen with sound, music and language options
* Interface translated into English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese
* Interface now scales properly on high resolution screens
