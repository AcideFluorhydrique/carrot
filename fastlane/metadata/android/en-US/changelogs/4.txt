Mark several obstacles at once.

Before, only one obstacle could be marked at a time. Once the wave passed, the
towers near other obstacles just sat idle unless you kept switching the mark
around by hand.

Now tap as many obstacles as you like. A small badge shows each mark's number.
Towers still shoot enemies first; with no enemy in range, they attack the
earliest marked obstacle they can reach. Tap a marked obstacle again to unmark
it, and the numbers after it move up.
